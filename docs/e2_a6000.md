# E2: the Stage 9 comparison on an RTX A6000

The cost model's accuracy on the RTX A6000 (sm_86) is similar to its accuracy on the RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, and slightly lower. Its pick reaches 0.68 to 0.86 of the autotuned best, clock-adjusted (geometric mean 0.78, vs 0.81 on Blackwell). Its ranking correlates weakly with measurement (Spearman -0.09 to 0.47). It makes the same systematic choice on both GPUs, 8x8 thread tiles in 32x64-class blocks, and that choice is not the best on either.

## Setup

The Stage 9 driver (`bench/stage9_costmodel.py`), configuration space (105 configurations), and 8 shapes were rerun unchanged with the target switched through environment variables: `TF_GPU_ARCH=sm_86` (`opt`/`llc -mcpu`, `ptxas -arch`), `TF_GPU_SMS=84`, and `TF_GPU_DRAM_BW=768e9`. The SM count was read from the device (`cuDeviceGetAttribute`). The DRAM bandwidth is the nominal A6000 figure, not a measurement; it enters only the model's DRAM floor. The model's other constants (128 FP32 lanes per SM, 32 shared-memory words per cycle, 600-cycle latency, and the assumption that operand re-reads hit in L2) were not changed. The A6000's L2 is 6 MiB rather than 128 MiB, so that last assumption is weaker here. Registers, shared memory, and blocks per SM come from `ptxas` and the occupancy API for sm_86.

## Observations

- The model's pick is never the autotuned best on the A6000 either; the best is ranked 24th to 66th of 105 by the model.
- The model's picks are of the same kind as on Blackwell: 8x8 thread tiles, in 32x64 blocks at 6 of 8 shapes on the A6000 and 7 of 8 on Blackwell. Its inputs differ between the GPUs only in SM count, DRAM bandwidth, and the `ptxas` and occupancy data. No configuration was DRAM-bound in the model on either GPU, so the assumed A6000 bandwidth did not affect any pick.
- The measured best differs between the GPUs. On the A6000, 8x8 thread tiles in 128x128 blocks are best at 4096^3 and 2560^3; on Blackwell, an 8x4 tile is best at every shape of 1536^3 and above. 8x4 is the best thread tile on average on both GPUs.
- As on Blackwell, the model underestimates every group's time, and 8x8 tiles the most (predicted/measured 0.32).
- On the A6000, the clock-adjusted fraction of peak is higher than on Blackwell at large shapes (58.0% vs 37.8% at 4096^3), and the median clock drops to 1275 MHz in some 4096^3 runs.

## Open questions

- The A6000's 128 FP32 lanes per SM include 64 that are shared with INT32 work, so the peak used for clock adjustment may overstate what FP32 FMA code can reach there. The model uses the same figure; it was not changed for E2.
- A6000 DRAM bandwidth was not measured; the nominal 768 GB/s was used.
